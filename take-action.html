Three Ways to Mobilize in Your Barangay

Tangible, community-oriented solutions that create immediate local resilience.

Mangrove & Urban Gardening

Support indigenous mangrove reforestation along estuaries to build natural seawalls that absorb storm energy and protect spawning fish.

Solar & Energy Independence

Advocate for rooftop solar installations in universities and community centers to build decentralized microgrids that survive typhoon blackouts.

Solid Waste & Estuary Defense

Organize neighborhood zero-waste education and trash interception traps in esteros before plastic debris reaches Manila Bay.

Voices From The Archipelago

Recent messages submitted by students, coastal volunteers, and youth advocates across the Philippines.

"During Typhoon Carina, our street in Marikina turned into a river within hours. But before rescue boats even arrived, neighbors had already pulled bamboo rafts together to carry our elderly to the third floor of the chapel. That is Tindig." — Carlos M., Marikina River Basin
"Our school closed for two weeks because the classroom heat index hit 50°C. We can't study when our foreheads are burning. Climate justice is an educational right!" — Angelica R., Grade 11 Student, Bulacan
"Planting bakawan with my classmates opened my eyes. What takes hours to plant protects our coast for thirty years. We will keep planting every month." — Jayvee T., Palawan Marine Youth Volunteer
