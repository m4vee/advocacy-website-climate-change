Why We Built TINDIG

TINDIG ("To Stand Firm / Rise") was created for our college Digital Interface Design capstone project. Our goal was to reject sterile, generic web templates in favor of a deeply intentional design language that mirrors the ecological and cultural reality of the Philippine archipelago.

Too often, climate discourse portrays Filipinos as helpless statistics or celebrates shallow resilience. We designed this interface to embody collective identity: when college students, mangrove planters, and jeepney commuters stand together, individual vulnerabilities converge into sovereign, collective power.

Our Three Guiding Pillars

01. Paninindigan (Conviction)

We stand with frontline communities facing storm surges and land subsidence, treating digital storytelling as an act of public advocacy.

02. Bayanihan (Collective Action)

Reframing sustainability away from isolated individual guilt toward communal organizing, mangrove planting, and solidarity.

03. Katotohanan (Climatic Truth)

Grounded in empirical scientific evidence from PAGASA and IPCC reports to counter disinformation and historical amnesia.
